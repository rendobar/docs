Describe an edit as a JSON timeline of tracks and clips, and `compose` renders it to a single video. Each clip can carry transforms, color, speed, opacity, blend modes, chroma key, and text. Clips on one track join with a transition. Every clip below is a real `compose` render.

## Transitions

A transition joins two clips on a track. Pass `{ "type": "transition", "transition": "<name>", "duration": <seconds> }` between them. `wipe` and `slide` also take `direction: "left" | "right" | "up" | "down"`.
