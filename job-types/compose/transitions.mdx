To join two clips on a track, put a transition between them: `{ "type": "transition", "transition": "<name>", "duration": <seconds> }`. `wipe` and `slide` also accept `direction: "left" | "right" | "up" | "down"`.

| Transition | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `crossfade` | A dissolves directly into B |
| `fade` | A dips through black, then B fades up |
| `wipe` | a hard edge sweeps across (directional) |
| `slide` | B slides in over A from an edge (directional) |
| `zoom` | B grows in from the center |
