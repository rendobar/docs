<Accordion title="How much does Rendobar cost?">
  Free plan ships with a one-time \$5 credit grant, no card required. Pro is \$9/mo and includes \$5/mo in credits. Studio is \$29/mo and includes \$20/mo in credits. Both add a 25% bonus on every credit purchase. Credit packs \$10 to \$100 on every plan. Credits never expire. Compose bills per minute of output video: \$0.04 on every plan, billed per second with a 15-second minimum per render. Pro adds compose at 1440p and GPU FFmpeg. Studio adds compose at 4K and 60 fps and 50 concurrent jobs. Every other job type bills per second of compute. Full breakdown: [Credits and billing](/concepts/credits), [compose pricing](/jobs/compose/pricing), or the [pricing page](https://rendobar.com/pricing/).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What can I do with Rendobar today?">
  Nine job types are live: `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` for any FFmpeg command and file inspection, `compose` to render a video from a JSON timeline, `compress.target` to hit a file size, `caption.burn` and `captions.animate` for subtitles, and `image.generate`, `image.edit` and `image.upscale` for images.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What video formats are supported?">
  Every FFmpeg-compatible container and codec for input. Output defaults to the extension in your trailing filename (`output.mp4` → MP4). Override with `params.outputFormat`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I drive Rendobar from an AI agent?">
  Yes. Connect any MCP-compatible agent to `https://api.rendobar.com/mcp` with your API key. Six typed MCP tools: `submit_job`, `get_job`, `list_jobs`, `probe_media`, `get_account`, `upload_media`. See [MCP overview](/mcp-server).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long do jobs take?">
  Seconds to minutes, depending on input size and command. Hard caps on processing time: 1 hour (Free), 9 hours (Pro and Studio). [Limits](/support/limits).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a rate limit?">
  Yes. 120 req/min (Free), 600 req/min (Pro), 1200 req/min (Studio). Applies to REST and MCP. Limit hit returns `429 RATE_LIMITED` with a `Retry-After` header.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if a job fails?">
  A failed compose render is free when the failure is on Rendobar's side. When it may come from your input, such as the render tool exiting on a bad file, it pays for the compute it used at 2x. Other failed jobs are charged for the compute they used, the same way successful jobs are. A job that never started is free. Rendobar has no auto-refund. If you believe a failure was caused by a Rendobar bug or platform issue, contact support and we will review for a discretionary refund. Inspect `error.code` and `error.message` on the job, and retry via `POST /jobs/{id}/retry` if appropriate. [Error codes](/support/errors).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I run custom FFmpeg commands?">
  Yes. That is the entire `ffmpeg` job type. Commands run through 8 security layers (flag whitelist, format/filter blocklists, sandbox isolation) before execution. [FFmpeg](/jobs/ffmpeg).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get push updates instead of polling?">
  Configure a [webhook](/guides/webhooks) endpoint on your organization. Rendobar POSTs signed JSON when jobs change status. The dashboard uses a WebSocket internally. Programmatic push is webhook-only.
</Accordion>
