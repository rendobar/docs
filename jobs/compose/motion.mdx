Any numeric field that moves takes a list of keyframes instead of a value. One shape covers a Ken Burns push, a fade, a colour change and ducking music under a voice.


Any numeric field that moves takes a list instead of a value: `position`, `scale`, `rotate`, `opacity`, `volume`, a shape's `color` and `radius`, a box's `size`, and any numeric effect parameter.

```json
"transform": {
  "scale": [
    { "time": "0%", "value": 1, "easing": "easeInOutSine" },
    { "time": "100%", "value": 1.12 }
  ]
}
```

- `time` is seconds from the clip's start, or a percent of its length, so `"100%"` means the last frame without knowing how long the clip is.
- `easing` shapes the move **from that keyframe to the next one**, so it goes on the first of a pair. The last keyframe's easing is never used.
- 33 easings: `step`, `linear`, `smooth`, and the `easeIn`, `easeOut` and `easeInOut` families over Sine, Quad, Cubic, Quart, Quint, Expo, Circ, Back, Elastic and Bounce.

A Ken Burns push-in is the example above. A fade is the same shape on `opacity`, and ducking music under a voice is the same shape on `volume`.

## Where keyframes are accepted

`transform.position`, `transform.scale`, `transform.rotate`, `transform.size`, clip `opacity`, clip `volume`, a shape's `color` and `radius`, and every effect parameter marked animatable in the [effect reference](/docs/jobs/compose/reference/effects).

## The 33 easings

`step` holds the value until the next keyframe. `linear` moves at a constant rate. `smooth` eases in and out gently. The remaining 30 are the `easeIn`, `easeOut` and `easeInOut` families over Sine, Quad, Cubic, Quart, Quint, Expo, Circ, Back, Elastic and Bounce.

Back, Elastic and Bounce overshoot the value you gave, which is the point of them, so leave room at the edges of the frame when you use one on `position`.
