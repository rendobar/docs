Every clip takes these fields, whatever it holds. Most live in `transform`. Each example changes one thing on the same shot and backdrop. Position, scale, rotation, size and the corner pin all take keyframes. See [Keyframes and easing](/docs/jobs/compose/motion).

`position` places the clip's centre by default, as a percent of the frame or in pixels. `anchor` picks a different point of the clip to place.


`scale` multiplies the clip's natural size. `size` gives the clip its own box, and [fit](#how-a-picture-fills-its-box) sets how the picture fills it. A box with `fit` set to `cover` turns a wide shot into a tall panel.

## Pinning to a surface

`corners` places a clip by four points in place of a position, to put footage on a screen or a sign in the shot. It does not combine with position, scale, rotate or fit. Keyframe the points to follow a moving surface.

## How a picture fills its box

`fit` sets what happens when a clip's box has a different shape from its picture, such as a wide photo in a phone-shaped box.

| `fit` | The picture |
| --- | --- |
| `contain` | Shows all of it, with bars where it does not reach. The default. |
| `cover` | Fills the box and cuts off what overflows. |
| `fill` | Stretches to the box and distorts. |
| `blur` | Shows all of it over a blurred copy of itself, with no bars. |

`blur` fills the whole frame around a clip that fills it, so it does not combine with position, size, scale or rotation.
