`scale` multiplies the clip's natural size. `size` gives it a box of its own, which the picture fills the way [fit](/docs/jobs/compose/output#how-a-picture-fills-its-box) says, so a box plus cover is how a wide shot becomes a tall panel.

## Pinning to a surface

`corners` is the other form of a transform: four points instead of a position. It puts footage on a screen or a sign in the shot, and it does not combine with position, scale, rotate or fit.
