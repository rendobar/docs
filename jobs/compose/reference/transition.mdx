A transition is a clip of its own, placed between the two clips it joins. There is no transition field on an ordinary clip.

| Field | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `transition` * | `"crossfade"` \| `"fade"` \| `"slide"` \| `"push"` \| `"wipe"` \| `"zoom"` \| `"none"` | crossfade dissolves A into B, fade dips through black, slide moves B in over A, push moves B in and A out together, wipe reveals B in place along an edge or a shape, zoom grows B from the center, none is a hard cut. |
| `direction` | `"left"` \| `"right"` \| `"up"` \| `"down"` | slide, push and an edge wipe: which way the picture travels. Default left. |
| `shape` | `"edge"` \| `"circle"` \| `"box"` \| `"barnDoorHorizontal"` \| `"barnDoorVertical"` | wipe only. edge (default) sweeps a straight line across in `direction`, circle opens an iris from the centre, box opens a rectangle from the centre, barnDoorVertical opens a vertical gap from the middle out to the sides, barnDoorHorizontal opens a horizontal gap up and down. |
| `map` | string | wipe only: a wipe of your own, drawn by a greyscale image (png or jpg) at the frame's shape. Black turns over to the next clip first and white last, so a left-to-right gradient is an edge wipe and a radial one an iris. Takes no `shape` or `direction`. |
| `duration` * | number | Overlap in seconds. 0.4-0.8 is the professional range. Leave the next clip's `start` out and it is placed this far before the clip before it ends. Two clips placed by hand blend over the overlap they have. over 0. |
| `type` * | `"transition"` | Marks this track item as a transition. Place it BETWEEN two clips; leave the second clip's `start` out and the engine overlaps them by `duration`. |

The kinds and what each looks like are on [Transitions](/docs/jobs/compose/transitions).

Fields marked `*` are required. Every table on this page is generated from the schema the API validates with, so it cannot drift from what a render accepts.
