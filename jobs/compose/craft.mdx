These are the rules working editors follow, written as compose fields. You need no editing experience to use them. AI agents get the same rules from the MCP compose reference.

## Pacing

Clip `length` sets the pace:

- Social: 0.8 to 3 seconds, with a visual change at least every 3 seconds
- Vlog: 2 to 6 seconds
- Tutorial: 5 to 15 seconds, cut where a step ends
- Cinematic: 4 to 8 seconds, with varied lengths

Shorten clips as the video builds, then hold the final shot about twice as long. Make the first clip the hook, with no fade-in or logo, and show or promise the payoff within 3 seconds. On social, 20 to 35 second videos have the highest completion rate.

To cut to music, one beat lasts 60 divided by BPM seconds. Cut every 2 or 4 beats, and on every beat only at the climax. At 120 BPM that gives 1 or 2 second clips.

## Text placement and size

On vertical video, keep all text inside x 8 to 92% and y 13.5 to 83%, clear of the platform's interface. Put captions at y 60 to 75%.

Sizes at 1080x1920:

- Titles: 135 to 190px
- Lower thirds: 70 to 80px
- Captions: 90 to 105px

Give all text a stroke (black, width 4 to 8), a shadow or a background box. Keep titles to 6 words or fewer and 2 lines at most. Pair one display font (Oswald, Anton, Bebas Neue) with one body font (Inter, Roboto).

## Color looks

Apply one look to every clip in the video:

| Look | `color` value |
| --- | --- |
| Warm sunset | `{ "temperature": 5000, "saturation": 1.2, "contrast": 1.1 }` |
| Cool corporate | `{ "temperature": 7500, "contrast": 1.15, "saturation": 0.95 }` |
| Moody cinematic | `{ "temperature": 6200, "saturation": 0.75, "contrast": 1.25, "gamma": 0.92 }` |
| Vintage faded | `{ "temperature": 5600, "contrast": 0.85, "saturation": 0.65, "brightness": 0.06 }` |
| Bright social | `{ "temperature": 6800, "saturation": 1.3, "gamma": 1.15 }` |
| Night | `{ "temperature": 11000, "saturation": 0.6, "gamma": 0.8, "brightness": -0.15 }` |

When faces are in frame, keep saturation at 0.7 to 1.3, hue within 8 degrees and temperature at 4500 to 12000K. To match two clips shot differently, fix brightness first, then temperature, then saturation.

## Photos need motion

A still photo in a video looks like a mistake. Give every image clip a slow zoom (a Ken Burns move): `"transform": { "position": { "x": "50%", "y": "50%" }, "scale": [{ "time": "0%", "value": 1, "easing": "easeInOutSine" }, { "time": "100%", "value": 1.12 }] }`.

For more on pacing and transitions, see [Transitions](/docs/jobs/compose/transitions). For levels, see [Audio](/docs/jobs/compose/audio).
