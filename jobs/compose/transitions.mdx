Two clips next to each other on a track cut straight from one to the other. A `transition` on the second clip blends them instead. It always describes how this clip arrives, so it goes on the second of the pair.

```json
{
  "asset": { "type": "video", "src": "https://example.com/b.mp4" },
  "transition": { "kind": "crossfade", "duration": 0.8 }
}
```

The blend is taken out of the two clips, not added to the timeline, so a transition never makes the render longer.

## The seven kinds

`crossfade` dissolves one picture into the other. `fade` goes through the background colour between them, which reads as a chapter break. `slide` moves the new clip in over the old one, `push` shoves the old one out of frame, and both take a `direction`. `wipe` reveals the new clip along a moving edge whose `shape` you choose. `zoom` scales into the new clip. `none` is the hard cut, and it is the default because it is what professional editors use for almost every join.

## Wipe shapes

`edge` is a straight line travelling in `direction`. `circle` opens from the centre, `box` opens as a rectangle, and `barnDoorHorizontal` and `barnDoorVertical` split from the middle outwards.

## How long it can be

A transition cannot be longer than either clip it joins. Ask for more and the render clamps it to what fits and the job carries a warning, so a template built for long clips still works when a short one arrives. [Validate](/docs/jobs/compose/output#check-it-before-you-render) reports the same warning before you spend anything.


## How to use them

Professionals cut. Keep crossfades under 10% of your joins and pick ONE decorative style (slide or zoom) per video, at section boundaries only.

| Use | Transition | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Almost every join | none (hard cut) | 0 |
| Time passes, mood softens | `crossfade` | 0.6 to 1.0s |
| Chapter break | `fade` | 1 to 2s |
| Energy, social | `slide`, `push` or `zoom` | 0.3 to 0.7s |

Every field is in the [transition reference](/docs/jobs/compose/reference/transition).
