Compose bills per second of the video you get back, not per second of compute. The rate is \$0.04 per minute of output video. It is the same on every plan and for every video your plan can render, and you can read the price before anything renders.

Each render bills at least 15 seconds, which is 1 cent. The price comes out of your [credit balance](/concepts/credits). The \$5 signup credit covers 125 minutes of compose output.

| Render | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 10 s (bills the 15 s minimum) | \$0.01 |
| 30 s | \$0.02 |
| 2 min | \$0.08 |
| 10 min | \$0.40 |

Frame size and animated graphics do not change the price. A 2-minute render costs the same at 720p, at 4K, or with kinetic text and 3D cards on it.

## What each plan renders

Plans differ in what they can render, not in what a minute costs.

| Plan | Renders up to |
| --- | --- |
| Free (pay-as-you-go) | 1080p at 30 fps, animated graphics included |
| Pro | 1440p at 30 fps. Animated graphics up to 1080p at 30 fps |
| Studio | 4K at 60 fps. Animated graphics up to 1080p at 30 fps |
| Enterprise | Animated graphics above 1080p or 30 fps, at a contract rate. Email `hello@rendobar.com` |

Animated graphics are kinetic text, 3D cards, HTML and Lottie motion, and anything else the compose engine draws as animated graphics. The frame counts by its pixels, so a portrait 1080x1920 video counts as 1080p.

Pro costs \$9 a month and includes \$5 of credit each month. Studio costs \$29 a month and includes \$20 of credit each month. Their discount on compose is the 25% bonus on every credit purchase, so \$25 of credit buys \$31.25. See [credits and billing](/concepts/credits#plans).

A video above your plan is refused at submit with `403 PLAN_LIMIT`, before anything renders or is charged. The message names the plan that renders it. Only video outputs (`mp4`, `webm`, `gif`) are checked. A `jpg`, `png` or `mp3` output renders on every plan.

`GET /account/capabilities` returns your plan's limits as `composeMaxPixels` and `composeMaxFps` for a plain edit, and `composeAnimatedMaxPixels` and `composeAnimatedMaxFps` for one with animated graphics.

## Failed renders

A render that fails on Rendobar's side is free. A job that never started is free.

When the failure may come from your input, such as the render tool exiting on a bad file, the job pays for the compute it used at 2x. Check `error.code` on the job and the [error codes](/support/errors) to tell which case you hit.

## Get the price before you render

`POST /compose/validate` returns the quote with the rest of its checks, and validating is free. It runs the plan check too: a video above your plan comes back as `valid: false` with a problem at `["output"]`. From an AI agent, the MCP tool `validate_compose` does the same.

<CodeGroup>

```ts SDK
const check = await client.compose.validate(params);
if (!check.valid) console.error(check.problems);
else console.log(check.price); // { perMinuteUsd: 0.04, minSeconds: 15 }
```

```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://api.rendobar.com/compose/validate \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer rb_YOUR_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "params": { "schemaVersion": 1, "output": { "format": "mp4", "size": "1080p" }, "timeline": { "tracks": [{ "clips": [{ "asset": { "type": "video", "src": "https://example.com/a.mp4" } }] }] } } }'
```

</CodeGroup>

A valid result carries `price`:

```json
"price": { "perMinuteUsd": 0.04, "minSeconds": 15 }
```

The charge is the video's length in minutes times `perMinuteUsd`, counted by the second, and never less than `minSeconds`.

Every other job type, [FFmpeg](/jobs/ffmpeg) included, bills per second of compute. See [how a job is priced](/concepts/credits#how-is-a-job-priced).
