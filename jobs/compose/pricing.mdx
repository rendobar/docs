Compose bills per second of the video you get back, not per second of compute. You can read the price before anything renders.

| Plan | Price per output minute |
| --- | --- |
| Free (pay-as-you-go) | \$0.04 |
| Pro | \$0.03 |
| Enterprise | Contract rate. Email `hello@rendobar.com` |

Each render bills at least 20 seconds. The price comes out of your [credit balance](/concepts/credits). The \$5 signup credit covers 125 minutes of compose output at the pay-as-you-go rate.

## What changes the price

Animated graphics bill at 3x. That covers kinetic text, 3D cards, HTML and Lottie motion, and anything else the compose engine draws as animated graphics.

Resolutions above 1080p are counted in 1080p frames, so a larger frame bills as more than one.

| Render | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 s at 1080p (bills the 20 s minimum) | \$0.013 | \$0.01 |
| 2 min at 1080p | \$0.08 | \$0.06 |
| 2 min at 1080p with animated graphics | \$0.24 | \$0.18 |

## Failed renders

A render that fails on Rendobar's side is free. A job that never started is free.

When the failure may come from your input, such as the render tool exiting on a bad file, the job pays for the compute it used at 2x. Check `error.code` on the job and the [error codes](/support/errors) to tell which case you hit.

## Get the price before you render

`POST /compose/validate` returns the quote with the rest of its checks, and validating is free. From an AI agent, the MCP tool `validate_compose` does the same.

<CodeGroup>

```ts SDK
const check = await client.compose.validate(params);
if (!check.valid) console.error(check.problems);
```

```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://api.rendobar.com/compose/validate \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer rb_YOUR_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "params": { "schemaVersion": 1, "output": { "format": "mp4", "size": "1080p" }, "timeline": { "tracks": [{ "clips": [{ "asset": { "type": "video", "src": "https://example.com/a.mp4" } }] }] } } }'
```

</CodeGroup>

Every other job type, [FFmpeg](/jobs/ffmpeg) included, bills per second of compute. See [how a job is priced](/concepts/credits#how-is-a-job-priced).
